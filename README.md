## Overview
Google organizes information. LLMs generate text. **UTEMPEA OS** validates medical truth[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). 

UTEMPEA OS is an autonomous pre-peer-review medical research verification engine and clinical claim auditing portal designed for independent biotech researchers, medical students, clinicians, and healthcare institutions.

## Core Verification Pillars (+1 Standard)
1. **Statistical Forensics:** Audits sample power, p-value coherence, and detects hidden p-hacking anomalies.
2. **First-Principles Biological Plausibility:** Evaluates hypothetical models and mechanisms of action against human physiology and biochemical laws.
3. **Protocol Alignment:** Cross-verifies control structures, blinding integrity, and clinical trial benchmarks.
4. **Asymmetry Triage:** Issues deterministic Green, Yellow, or Red clearance signals with a traceable `UTMP-2026` badge ID and downloadable HTML clearance certificate.

## Three User Layers
* **Researchers Layer:** Pre-peer-review verification for research papers, theses, and theoretical models without institutional gatekeeping[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
* **Doctors Layer:** Instant evidence-linked medical claim verification and AI/LLM hallucination detection[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Patients Layer:** Structured, noise-free clinical information reports to discuss with a licensed physician.
